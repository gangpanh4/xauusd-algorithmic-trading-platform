# Project Roadmap

> This roadmap outlines the planned development of the XAUUSD Algorithmic Trading Platform. It serves as a high-level guide for future milestones and priorities. The roadmap may evolve as research, testing, and trading performance provide new insights.

---

# Vision

Build a professional, modular, research-driven algorithmic trading platform capable of progressing from historical research to reliable live trading while maintaining high standards of software engineering.

The long-term objective is **consistent profitability through measurable, data-driven improvements**.

# Guiding Principles

Development priorities are determined by the following order:

1. Correctness
2. Reliability
3. Maintainability
4. Trading performance
5. Scalability
6. Performance optimization

New features should only be added when they provide measurable value or support the long-term vision of the platform.

The roadmap represents the planned evolution of the XAUUSD Algorithmic Trading Platform from a research-focused development environment into a professional, production-ready algorithmic trading system.
